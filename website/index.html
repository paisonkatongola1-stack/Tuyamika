One ecosystem · four experiences

Give. Celebrate. Discover. Deliver.

Tuyamika means "thank you". Built for individuals, families, committees and businesses who celebrate together.

🎁

Gift Registries

Personalised wish lists for weddings, Chilanga Mulilo, kitchen parties, baby showers and every milestone. Shareable by link or QR. Guests reserve gifts without duplicate purchases, with surprise privacy built in.

🤝

Group Gifting

Committees pool contributions towards catering, décor or an expensive gift. Live progress meters per goal, private contributor amounts, treasurer-grade financial records.

🎉

Event Workspaces

Guest lists, RSVPs, tasks, budgets and committee roles — chairperson, treasurer, secretary — each with just the right permissions. Suppliers discovered and quoted in one place.

🛍️

Marketplace & Specials

Shop curated products from trusted Zambian and Zimbabwean retailers, request quotes from event professionals, or send a ready-made Tuyamika Special. Payment coordinated, delivered with care.

The app

Designed warm. Built practical.

Contemporary African heritage — bold, modern, joyful. Android and iOS apps plus mobile web for your guests.
